## PB-2.0 — Multi-Year Ramp Pricing Rules

- **PB-2.1 Committed Term Requirement:** Multi-year discounts exceeding `10.0%` require a **firm, non-cancellable 36-month Initial Term**.
- **PB-2.2 Ramp Guardrail:** Annual seat ramps up to `2.0x` Year-1 seat count (e.g., 50 -> 70 -> 80 seats) are permitted without exception only when Years 2 and 3 are firm minimum commitments.
- **PB-2.3 Seat True-Down Prohibition:** Downward seat adjustment clauses ("true-downs") in Years 2–3 are **prohibited** on discounted ramps unless approved in writing by Finance Controller and Revenue Accounting.

## PB-4.0 — Gross Margin Floor & Mandatory Margin Sign-Off Controls

- **PB-4.1 Enterprise Gross Margin Floor:** Every enterprise transaction must achieve a minimum consolidated **3-Year Gross Margin of `75.0%`** (`(Net TCV - Total Direct COGS) / Net TCV >= 75.0%`).
- **PB-4.2 Margin Exception Procedure:** Any transaction falling below `75.0%` Gross Margin requires a formal **Finance Margin Exception Packet** routed through Deal Desk and approved in writing by the **Finance Controller (Elena Rostova)** or **CFO** prior to Legal contract redlining.
- **PB-4.3 Verbal Approval Prohibition (Zero-Exception Control):** **Verbal approvals, hallway conversations, forecast-call nods, and casual Slack acknowledgments that do not attach and confirm the final P&L, true discount %, and margin % are strictly INVALID and do not satisfy governance or SOX audit controls.**

## PB-5.0 — Non-Standard Contract, Payment & Revenue Recognition Controls

- **PB-5.1 Standard Payment Terms:** Standard enterprise payment terms are **Net-30 from invoice date, billed annually in advance upon Order Form Effective Date**.
  - `Net-45` or `Net-60` from invoice date requires **written Finance Controller approval**.
  - **Acceptance-gated invoicing** (starting the payment clock after customer-signed Go-Live Acceptance) requires **written Revenue Accounting (ASC 606) + CFO approval** because it defers revenue recognition and extends Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) beyond 90–120 days.
- **PB-5.2 Implementation Billing Split:** Standard billing is `100%` upfront on Effective Date. Any milestone/acceptance holdback on services requires VP Services + Revenue Accounting sign-off.
- **PB-5.3 Termination for Convenience / Custom Opt-Out Prohibition:**
  - Unilateral termination for convenience ("opt-out") clauses are **strictly prohibited** on multi-year contracts carrying discounts `> 10.0%` unless granted a joint written exception by **General Counsel + CFO + Revenue Accounting**.
  - Under **ASC 606**, a penalty-free opt-out at Month 12 reduces the enforceable contract term to 12 months, prohibiting booking or recognizing Years 2–3 TCV as committed backlog.
  - Any approved opt-out on a discounted multi-year ramp MUST include either (a) a retroactive discount clawback to standard 1-year list price (`$1,500/seat`) or (b) an early termination fee >= `50%` of remaining unbilled contract value.
- **PB-5.4 Liability Cap, SLA & MFN Guardrails:**
  - Standard Liability Cap: `12 months' fees paid` (any increase > `1x` or uncapped data/security carve-out requires General Counsel + CFO approval).
  - Standard Renewal Uplift: `7%` annual cap (`0%` multi-year renewal caps require Deal Desk + Finance approval).
  - SLA Termination-for-Cause w/ Refund and Most Favored Nation (MFN) clauses are **strictly prohibited** without General Counsel + CFO sign-off.

## PB-6.0 — System Hygiene & Commission Governance

- **PB-6.1 Three-Way Match Rule:** Prior to Legal turning customer redlines, Deal Desk must verify an exact three-way match across **(a) CRM Opportunity Record**, **(b) Final Quote Spreadsheet**, and **(c) Draft Order Form**. Any mismatch triggers an automatic **HARD HOLD**.
- **PB-6.2 Commission Protection:** Sales commissions paid upfront on multi-year TCV are subject to 100% clawback on uncollected Years 2–3 if a customer exercises an opt-out or true-down clause.
