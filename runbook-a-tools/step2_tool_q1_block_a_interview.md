# Runbook A — Step 2: Q1 Block A Structured Interview Protocol & Capture Form

## Session Pacing (Step 2 Total = 30 Minutes)
- **Minutes 0–15:** Run **Q1 Block A Structured Interview** (Who / When / What deal shape) using the verbatim probes below.

## Non-Negotiable Evidence Rule
Every answer given during the interview is tagged **CLAIMED** until matched to a specific line or cell in the five Step 1 raw artifacts (`a` CRM, `b` Quote Sheet, `c` Redlined Contract, `d` Approval Thread, `e` Price Book & Policy). Anything absent from primary sources is tagged **`UNVERIFIED`**.

## BLOCK A1 — WHO (Real Decision-Maker / Economic Buyer vs Champion vs Internal Blockers)

### Verbatim Clinician Questions & Primary-Source Probes
1. **Economic Buyer Identity:** "Who has unilateral budget and signature authority for this contract amount — and what primary-source document proves their authority?"
   - *Probe if champion is named:* "Look at the contract signature block (`Artifact c`) and procurement/legal emails (`Artifact d`). Whose name is on the signatory line?"
2. **Direct Access Check:** "What was the date of our last direct meeting or email with that economic buyer?"
   - *Probe if zero direct meetings:* "Then we flag direct economic-buyer engagement as `VERIFIED ABSENT` and their buying criteria as `UNVERIFIED`."
3. **Internal Approval Holders:** "Who inside our company currently owns the next required commercial, margin, or legal sign-off — and has the packet reached their inbox?"

## BLOCK A2 — WHEN (Stall Onset, Last Verified Event & Hard Deadlines)

### Verbatim Clinician Questions & Primary-Source Probes
1. **Exact Stall Onset:** "On what exact calendar date did forward contracting momentum stop — and what document arrived or stalled on that date?"
2. **Last Verified External Event:** "Show me the timestamp of the last verified email, redline, or logged interaction from the buyer. Who is waiting on whom right now?"
3. **Last Verified Internal Event:** "Show me the timestamp of the last internal Deal Desk, Legal, or Finance ticket entry. How many days has the internal action item sat unaddressed?"
4. **Hard External Deadline:** "Is there a verified buyer-side date (budget committee, renewal cliff, go-live window) documented in writing — or is the close date only an internal quarter-end target?"

## BLOCK A3 — WHAT DEAL SHAPE (Price, Term, Structure, Controls & Approvals)

### Verbatim Clinician Questions & Primary-Source Probes
1. **Price & True Discount:** "What is the headline value, the standard price book list total, the subscription discount %, and are there any manual overrides on services or add-ons?"
2. **Margin & Unit Economics:** "What is the consolidated gross margin % on the live pricing sheet versus our policy margin floor?"
3. **Term & Exit Structure:** "Is the contract genuinely non-cancellable for the full term, or does the latest redline include an opt-out, termination for convenience, or seat true-down?"
4. **Billing & Payment Terms:** "When can we legally invoice, and when does the payment clock start — contract signature or customer acceptance?"
5. **Written Approvals vs Verbal Support:** "Which approvals required by our written Discount Policy (`Artifact e`) exist in writing on the final P&L, and which are verbal-only or missing?"
