# Runbook A — Step 4: Disconfirming-Evidence Hunt Protocol & Falsification Worksheet

## Clinical Purpose
Humans naturally hunt for **confirming** evidence. In Step 4, the clinician explicitly hunts for **disconfirming evidence** — exact lines, numbers, or timestamps in Step 1 raw artifacts (`a–e`) that **contradict**:
1. **Part A — The Prospect's / Buyer's Story** (claims made by the buyer champion, buyer legal, or procurement about budget readiness, commitment, urgency, or "standard terms").
2. **Part B — The AE's / Internal Story** (claims made by the AE or sales leadership about who is stalling, what was approved, or forecast readiness).

## PART A — HUNTING FOR EVIDENCE THAT CONTRADICTS THE PROSPECT'S STORY

### Clinical Probes to Break Buyer Narratives
1. **Budget vs Reality:** Does buyer champion claim Q4 readiness while buyer legal, procurement, or signature blocks reveal unapproved capital budget?
2. **Commitment vs Escape Hatch:** Does buyer ask for multi-year volume pricing while inserting an opt-out, zero clawback, or seat true-down?
3. **"Standard Terms" vs Risk Transfer:** Does buyer counsel label a redline "standard procurement policy" while demanding asymmetric liability caps, uncapped carve-outs, MFN, or subjective acceptance gates?
4. **Authority vs Access:** Does the champion promise a fast signature while blocking direct access to the sole legal signatory?

## PART B — HUNTING FOR EVIDENCE THAT CONTRADICTS THE AE'S / INTERNAL STORY

### Clinical Probes to Break Internal Narratives
1. **Who Stalled?** Does the approval-hop timestamp audit confirm or falsify the claim that buyer legal or internal bureaucracy caused the delay?
2. **Valid Approval?** Does the raw approval thread contain written approval on the final P&L, or only casual verbal/Slack nods invalid under written policy?
3. **Clean Pricing?** Does the live Excel spreadsheet match CRM fields, or reveal higher discounts, overridden list cells, and sub-floor margins?
4. **Forecast Integrity?** Does CRM hold the deal in `Commit` despite active Deal Desk / Legal hard holds or unengaged economic buyers?
