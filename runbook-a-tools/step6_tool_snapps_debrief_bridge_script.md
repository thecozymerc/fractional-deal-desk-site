Read **ONLY** verified facts, non-overlapping financial figures, and properly scoped contract counts that **survived the Step 5 Diagnostic Time-Out**. Keep every unverified unknown explicitly tagged `UNVERIFIED`.

## 30-Minute 4-Beat SNAPPS Choreography

### Beat 1 — Problem Representation: Align on Verified Facts (Minutes 0–5)
- **Goal:** Get every executive in the room (CRO, CFO, VP RevOps, Legal) to nod yes to the exact same verified baseline before touching conclusions.
- **Verbatim Script:**
  > "Before we discuss why this deal stalled or what options you have, let's take 90 seconds to align strictly on verified facts from your own primary-source documents — zero hearsay, zero opinion."
  - *(Read the verified 60–90 second UW FCM Problem Representation verbatim.)*
  - **Alignment Check:** *"Does anything in those verified facts conflict with a primary document you hold — or are we 100% aligned on the factual baseline?"*

### Beat 2 — Exact Stuck Point & Failed Controls: Cause X vs Cause Y (Minutes 5–15)
- **Goal:** Prove the exact stuck point, non-overlapping dollar leak/exposure, failed controls with artifact proof, and why the root cause is **Cause X** and NOT just **Cause Y**.
- **Structure (Always in This Order):**
  1. **Refute Cause Y (The Comfortable Myth):** Show the Approval-Hop Timestamp Audit and Disconfirming-Evidence Hunt proving why the AE's story (or single-person scapegoating) is false.
  2. **Prove Cause X (The Real Mechanism):** Point directly to exact document lines, spreadsheet cells, and timestamps across the failed internal controls AND any unengaged external buyer gate.
  3. **Quantify Non-Overlapping Exposure (Waterfall Only):** State Price Concession Leak, Gross Margin Floor Deficit, ASC 606 Enforceable TCV Collapse, Year-1 Net Cash Contribution, and separate Contingent Legal Risk.
  4. **State Explicit Scope Limits (`UNVERIFIED`):** Name any unpulled documents (e.g., base MSA, SOW) and unverified unknowns upfront — this builds instant CFO credibility.

### Beat 3 — Clean Boundary (No Rescue): Client-Owned MESO Structural Options (Minutes 15–23)
- **Goal:** Hand the client 3 financially modeled **MESO** (Multiple Equivalent Simultaneous Offers) packages plus a Controlled Deferral option — while holding an ironclad **No-Rescue Boundary**.
- **Verbatim Boundary Line:**
  > "Here is our clean boundary: we do not step into your deal to redline contracts, call your prospect's CFO, or negotiate with your Finance team. The diagnosis and the decision belong to you. What we *have* modeled are three policy-grounded structural MESO options — plus a controlled de-commit option — so your team can choose which trade-off to put on the table."
- Walk cleanly through **Option A**, **Option B**, **Option C**, and **Option D** from `step6_tool_meso_options_calculator.xlsx`, highlighting which internal approvals each option requires.

### Beat 4 — The Commercial Bridge: Validation Mode Slot #1 -> 5-Deal Forensic Diagnostic (Minutes 23–30)
- **Goal:** Transition cleanly from proving **one** broken deal to quantifying systemic deal-flow leakage across **five** deals in **5 business days**.
- **Verbatim Commercial Bridge (Deliver Word-for-Word):**
  > "We proved exactly why this deal broke. What neither of us knows yet is whether this is a one-off outlier or how 20-30% of your deal flow quietly leaks across handoffs. That's what the 5-deal Forensic Diagnostic proves in 5 business days — with this deal as Slot #1."
- **Slot Selection Close:**
  > "If you want to know by this time next week whether [ACCOUNT] is an isolated event or a repeatable 20–30% pipeline leak, we pull 4 additional deals — two late-stage stalled or heavy-discount commits, one recently won ramp, and one recent closed-lost — and run the exact same clinical method across Slots #2 through #5 in 5 business days."
- **Pricing Governance Note (Unruled Suggestion):**
  - *Note: Neon's suggestion of a `$100` validation fee credited toward the 5-deal Forensic Diagnostic is **unruled and optional — NOT part of the ruled price**. State only the client's ruled commercial terms unless explicitly authorized otherwise.*
