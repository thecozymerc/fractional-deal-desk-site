# Runbook A — Step 5: Mandatory Diagnostic Time-Out Script & No-Rescue Playbook (10 Min)

## Clinical Purpose
Before building a single slide or bullet of the Step 6 SNAPPS Executive Readout, **STOP EVERYTHING for 10 minutes**. Just as a surgical team runs a pre-incision time-out and clinicians run Ely, Graber, and TWED cognitive checkpoints to prevent diagnostic errors, Step 5 catches anchoring, single-cause scapegoating, uncited claims, double-counted dollar math, and deal-rescue temptation before they reach leadership.

---

## Minute-by-Minute 10-Minute Facilitation Script

### Minutes 0:00–1:30 — Call the Hard Pause
> "Stop pens and close the readout draft. We are taking a mandatory 10-minute Diagnostic Time-Out using Ely's Time-Out, Graber's Diagnostic Safety Checklist, and TWED. Our job right now is to try to **break** our own Step 3 leading hypothesis and Step 4 forensic findings before we present them. Finding an error here in 10 minutes protects our credibility in Step 6."

### Minutes 1:30–4:00 — Rapid Ely & Graber Cognitive / System Scan
Ask these 5 hard questions aloud:
1. **Ely Completeness Check:** "Does our leading hypothesis actually account for *all* verified evidence — or did we ignore an inconvenient external buyer fact or internal tooling failure?"
2. **Graber Attribution Check:** "Are we scapegoating the AE because their hop share is high, while ignoring the broken CRM/CPQ controls that let an unapproved quote leave the building?"
3. **Graber Missing-Document Check:** "Which governing documents were *not* in Step 1 (e.g., base MSA, signed SOW, phone logs)? Have we explicitly marked those clauses as `UNVERIFIED` rather than claiming we audited them?"
4. **Graber Framing Check:** "Is quarter-end Commit urgency making us over-weight 'getting it signed' over post-signature margin, rev-rec, and liability damage?"
5. **Graber False-Green Check:** "Did we call out every automated system field that falsely showed green/approved?"

### Minutes 4:00–8:30 — Mandatory 4-Letter TWED Interrogation
Run every letter out loud and log every catch as `PASS` or `FAIL CAUGHT -> REMEDIATED`:
- **T (Threat):** *"What blows up post-signature if this closes as written?"* — Require exact ASC 606, Year-1 cash/GM, commission clawback, and legal liability figures.
- **W (What else?):** *"Did I accept the AE's framing? What else explains the stall?"* — Force explicit check of BOTH internal tooling/governance failure AND external buyer non-qualification/non-budget.
- **E (Evidence):** *"Does every finding tie to a specific document line or timestamp?"* — Audit every headline number destined for Step 6; strip any non-additive summed exposures and tag every unverified assumption `UNVERIFIED`.
- **D (Dispositional):** *"Am I getting sucked into rescuing the deal instead of diagnosing it?"* — Check whether looming customer deadlines are tempting the clinician to redline clauses, email buyer counsel, or lobby Finance.

### Minutes 8:30–10:00 — Lock Remediations & Issue Step 6 Release Gate
- Confirm **ZERO** unresolved `FAIL - BLOCKING` controls remain.
- Confirm every caught item has a written **Step 6 Correction / Guardrail**.
- Read the Gate aloud: *"Time-Out complete. All catches remediated. Cleared to build Step 6 SNAPPS Executive Readout."*

## 5 Classic Step 5 Rescue & Bias Traps — Verbatim Deflection Scripts

| Trap Raised During Time-Out | Clinical Error Violated | Verbatim Clinician Deflection |
|---|---|---|
| *"We're running behind — let's skip the time-out and jump straight to the executive readout."* | Ely / Graber Safety Bypass | "Skipping the 10-minute time-out is how uncited claims and double-counted risk numbers sneak in front of a CFO. Ten minutes here saves weeks of damaged credibility." |
| *"Their CFO budget committee is in 6 days — can you jump on the call with us or draft the redline counter?"* | **TWED `D` (Dispositional)** | "No — stepping into the negotiation breaks our diagnostic boundary and turns us into deal rescue. In Step 6 we will lay out clean MESO structural options for your own team to take to Finance and the buyer." |
| *"Look at the hop audit — 85% was the AE. Let's just tell leadership this is an AE discipline issue."* | **Graber `C5` & TWED `W`** | "That fails our Attribution and 'What Else?' check. Why did CRM let a rep leave discount at 20%, auto-clear Finance, overwrite cell E13, and stay in Commit on a HARD HOLD? We report the failed controls AND the external CFO gap — not just one person." |
| *"The Three-Way Diff sums to $1.3M+ in exposure — let's put '$1.3M at risk' on the title slide!"* | **TWED `E` (Evidence)** | "Stop — those line exposures overlap and are non-additive. Putting $1.3M in front of Finance fails our Evidence check. We only report clean, non-overlapping Waterfall figures ($90K concession, $10K GM breach, $162K unenforceable TCV, $17K Y1 net) plus separate contingent liability." |
| *"We ran the 51-flag engine, so let's tell execs we completed a full MSA & Order Form legal audit."* | **Graber `S1` & TWED `E`** | "That fails our Missing-Artifact check. Four base-MSA flags (consequential damages, custom IP ownership, cyber insurance, audit rights) were outside the Order Form excerpt and must be explicitly labeled `UNVERIFIED — FULL MSA CHECK REQUIRED`." |
