Proof of Work · Spend Variance

Spend Variance & Renewal Tracker.

Unmanaged vendor contracts leak money quietly: spend drifts above forecast, invoices arrive that do not match the PO, and renewal notice dates pass while nobody is watching. This tracker is the control system for all three — combining a contract register, a monthly forecast-versus-actual tracker, an invoice audit log, and a live roll-up that flags what needs a decision.

The model behind this tool is the operator’s own working contract spend and renewal tracker, rebuilt as a clean web template. The operator brings 15+ years in commercial contracts, deal desk, and revenue operations and has governed contracts up to $300M. The deals and figures loaded in the tool are fictional samples.
