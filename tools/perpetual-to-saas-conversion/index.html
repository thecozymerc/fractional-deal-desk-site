Proof of Work · Pricing Engines

Perpetual-to-SaaS Conversion Calculator.

When a customer is deciding whether to stay on a perpetual license or move to subscription pricing, this calculator compares staying put with three SaaS options, year by year. It shows both the cumulative break-even and the year when the annual run-rate crosses over, with a chart so the shape of the decision is visible.

Below is the actual workbook — its one sheet, with its chart. The names and data are samples where they need to be, but the mechanics are real: change an input, and the tool answers. The formulas stay under the hood. Each SaaS option is priced the real way — a list price per seat per month and a discount give the net PUPM; a starting seat count and a yearly growth rate grow the deal — and each option can also carry a one-time coupon credit and a usage line, priced per use or per token. You can rename each option, too — the sheet calls it by your name everywhere below. So the sheet shows both stories at once: what the conversion saves per seat, and why the total can grow as the rollout grows. A seats-by-year table sits under the cost table, so you can watch each option’s seats grow year by year — or hold flat, when its growth is set to zero — and a cost-per-seat line divides each option’s total by the seats it serves in the final year. In this sample, the third option stays flat while the first two grow.
