Tier 3 — Specialized & Channel Frameworks (Back Drawer).

The five specialized and channel forms, kept one layer down for depth — each with the analyst note: what I check and redline on that kind of paper before it moves.

Distribution License Agreement (VAR)

What I check & redline: reseller discount schedules, territory boundaries, minimum sales thresholds, warranty pass-through limits, and audit rights over partner sales reporting.

Hybrid Cloud Agreement with Professional Services
