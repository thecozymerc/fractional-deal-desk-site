S6-P01.a — Pricing Engines

Deal Pricing Solver

Most discount fights are one triangle argued for days: list price, discount, and partner margin. Give the solver any two and it solves the third. It also includes price-per-user-per-month math and a multi-year block that shows year-by-year discounts, total contract value, retained margin after the partner’s share, and whether the deal stays inside the margin floor you set.

Below is the working calculator built from that model — broader than the sheet it came from. Price a new deal line by line: products from the dropdowns (rename any line to the customer's own products), seats and per-use or per-token usage side by side, coupons on any line, and an extra concession that kicks in when usage reaches the level you set. Then flip to the second tab and run the same deal as old versus new. The sample names and numbers are samples where they need to be, but the mechanics are real: change anything yellow, and every result on the page answers. The formulas stay under the hood.
