Deal Pricing Solver

Most discount fights are one triangle argued for days: list price, discount, and partner margin. Give the solver any two and it solves the third. It also includes price-per-user-per-month math and a multi-year block that shows year-by-year discounts, total contract value, retained margin after the partner's share, and whether the deal stays inside the margin floor you set.

Provenance & Operating Record: Built from 15+ years in commercial contracts, deal desk, and revenue operations across contracts up to $300M and 3,000+ SKUs under management. All numbers preloaded here are generic sample values for demonstration only — rename any product or usage line, adjust list prices, seats, discounts, partner margins, coupons, and usage thresholds to model a real deal.

How the working calculator runs

Two views · seat & usage lines · coupons · threshold concession · SKU-mix check

Price a new deal line by line: products from the dropdowns (rename any line to the customer's own products), seats and per-use or per-token usage side by side, coupons on any line, and an extra concession that kicks in when usage reaches the level you set. Core identity, rounding rules & intentional omissions

      

      

        

          
How the solver & line math connect

          
    
            
  1. Triangle identity: Net PUPM = List × (1 − Discount) × (1 − Partner Margin) = ARPU ÷ Months. Fix any two variables in the flat matrix or smart form and the third solves automatically off the baseline Net PUPM.
    2. 
            
  2. Product lines & coupons: Each product line prices seats in months (default 36), computes Net PUPM, Net ARPU (PUPY), ACV, and TCV, and spreads any flat Coupon ($) across the line's years on ACV while deducting the full coupon from TCV.
    3. 
            
  3. Usage & concession: Billed units = max(expected units, committed minimum). When billed units on the selected meter meet or exceed the threshold, the extra discount reduces that meter's monthly net usage and feeds the full-deal TCV.
    4. 
          

        

        

          
Sample boundaries & standing omissions

          

            Display & zero guards: Currency outputs display to two decimal places; per-token prices display to eight decimal places. Empty inputs evaluate as zero, and any zero-divisor condition renders cleanly as "—" rather than NaN or Infinity.
          

          

            Model scope: Every product name, price, discount, margin, usage rate, and SKU-mix rule on this page is a generic sample value from the model documentation. An organization's commercial policy may differ; nothing here constitutes pricing or legal advice.
