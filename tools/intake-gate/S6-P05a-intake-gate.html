Intake Gate & Submission Scorecard

This deal desk intake gate scores each incoming submission against five baseline checks and routes it by rule: clean deals fast-track, incomplete deals are held at the gate and returned to Sales with a pushback note, and any deal marked Approved without its baseline checks or above the discount threshold fires an immediate discount approval audit alert.

Built directly from the operator’s intake gate workbook, this tool reflects 15+ years in commercial contracts, deal desk, and revenue operations—commercial contracts up to $300M, and pricing catalogs of 3,000+ SKUs under management. All sample deals and accounts pre-loaded below are fictional.

How the Deal Intake Gate & Discount Approval Audit Works

        Workbook Logic & Baseline Checks
      

      

        

          
How It Works

          

            For each deal, enter the deal type, proposed ACV, and requested discount, then answer the five baseline checks Yes or No: account verified, prior contract linked, eligibility confirmed, SKU mix valid, docs complete. The Readiness Score is the share of Yes answers. Set the approval status — and if a deal is marked Approved before all five checks pass, or with a discount over 20%, the gate fires an AUDIT ALERT and routes the deal to STOP – REVOKE & AUDIT. Clean deals fast-track; incomplete deals are held and returned to Sales with a pushback note explaining what is missing.
          

          
    
            
  • Readiness %: (Y answers among the 5 checks) / 5.
    • 
            
  • Flag: Status = "Approved" AND (Readiness < 100% OR Discount > 20%) → AUDIT ALERT: Approved Without Baseline; else OK.
    • 
            
  • Routing: Alert → STOP – REVOKE & AUDIT; Readiness < 100% → HOLD – RETURN TO SALES; Discount ≤ 15% → FAST-TRACK (Pre-Approved); else → ROUTE TO DEAL DESK – FINANCE.
    • 
          

        

        

          
What the Five Baseline Checks Verify

          
    
            
  • Account Verified: Legal entity name, billing address, and CRM account hierarchy match the order form.
    • 
            
  • Prior Contract Linked: Executed MSA, active order forms, and prior amendments are linked on renewals, expansions, and mid-term resets.
    • 
            
  • Eligibility Confirmed: Volume tier minimums, multi-year ramp terms, or promotional rules are satisfied.
    • 
            
  • SKU Mix Valid: Required base licenses, implementation packages, and support SKUs are paired without deprecated or conflicting items.
    • 
            
  • Docs Complete: Redlines, exception approvals, and supporting schedules are attached before commercial sign-off.
