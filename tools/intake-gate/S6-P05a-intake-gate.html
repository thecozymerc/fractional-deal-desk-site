Intake Gate & Submission Scorecard.

When enterprise deals stall, the bottleneck is rarely a complex strategy problem. Most delays happen because sales teams bring deals across the fence without doing the baseline work — missing paperwork, missing information, incomplete or unverified accounts, unconfirmed renewal eligibility, or missing approvals — expecting Deal Desk to act as their administrative buffer. When Deal Desk accommodates incomplete submissions, sales learns to treat deal operations like an administrative concierge, and the deal drops into an endless operational loop between Deal Desk, Finance, and Fulfillment. This is the front-door control gate that stops that: five baseline checks on every deal, a live readiness score, an audit flag that catches approvals that skipped the baseline, and a dashboard that tracks how clean the queue really is. Every deal ID, account name, and figure in it is sample data.

Directions: For each deal, enter the deal type, proposed ACV, and requested discount, then answer the five baseline checks Yes or No: account verified, prior contract linked, eligibility confirmed, SKU mix valid, docs complete. The Readiness Score is the share of Yes answers. Set the approval status — and if a deal is marked Approved before all five checks pass, or with a discount over 20%, the gate fires an AUDIT ALERT and routes the deal to STOP – REVOKE & AUDIT. Clean deals fast-track; incomplete deals are held and returned to Sales with a pushback note explaining what is missing.

Inputs, logic, limits

Inputs: ACV, discount %, the five Y/N checks, and approval status — nothing else. There are no contract-terms or customer-commitment inputs beyond those. Logic: readiness percentage, the audit-alert condition (Approved + readiness under 100% or discount over 20%), the four-way routing decision, and dashboard KPI counts. Limits: sample data only. Scores and routing move only when the deal-row inputs above change.

Story 1 — The gate that keeps the queue honest.

What this piece shows. This two-tab workbook is the front-door control gate for Deal Desk. It checks five binary baseline requirements on every submission: Account Verified, Prior Contract Linked, Eligibility Confirmed, SKU Mix Valid, and Required Docs Complete. It calculates a live Readiness Score (0% to 100%) for each deal. It runs an audit formula that catches unauthorized approvals — flagging any deal marked Approved in the system that bypassed baseline readiness checks or discount thresholds. It auto-generates the exact pushback note to Sales listing what is missing, so Deal Desk analysts do not waste time typing manual follow-up emails.

How a buyer uses it. A founder or revenue leader puts this gate in front of the deal approval queue. When a rep submits a deal, the sheet checks the basics first. If all five baseline checks pass, the formula routes the deal to Fast-Track (Pre-Approved) or to Deal Desk / Finance review based on the discount tier. If any baseline item is missing, the sheet stamps the deal “HOLD – RETURN TO SALES” and outputs the exact missing items. The Triage Dashboard tracks First-Pass Yield — the percentage of deals submitted clean on day one — alongside hold counts and audit alerts.

The point it proves. Real deal velocity only happens when Deal Desk enforces strict boundaries from day one. You do not speed up closing cycles by having Deal Desk chase missing fields for sales reps. You speed them up by requiring a clean structure at intake before a deal ever moves to approval.
