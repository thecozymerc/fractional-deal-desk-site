The Diagnostic Scorecard

Five QTC stages · One-tap Maturity (0–3) & Risk Weight (1–3)

How It Works

Work through the five stages — Intake, Pricing, Approvals, Redlining, and Billing & Renewals. For each stage's control point, score how mature the control is today (0 to 3) and how much risk it carries (1 to 3). The scorecard multiplies maturity by risk weight, flags the weakest high-risk controls P1 — fix first — and rolls everything into one readiness percentage on the dashboard. Raise a stage's maturity score and watch its flag clear and overall readiness climb.

Control Maturity Scale (0 to 3) 0 Ad-hoc / Uncontrolled · 1 Manual / Hero-dependent · 2 Documented / Partial · 3 Enforced System Control
Risk Weight Scale (1 to 3) 1 Low Commercial Exposure · 2 Moderate Process Risk · 3 High Revenue / Margin / Legal Risk

Workbook Scoring Rules

          
Every metric on this page is computed directly from the five stage inputs using the workbook's deterministic rules:

          
    
            
  • Weighted Score = Maturity × Risk Weight
    • 
            
  • Max Score = 3 × Risk Weight
    • 
            
  • Health % = Weighted Score ÷ Max Score (rounded)
    • 
            
  • Maturity ≤ 1 & Risk = 3 → P1 - CRITICAL FIX FIRST
    • 
            
  • Maturity ≤ 1 & Risk < 3 → P2 - FIX NEXT
    • 
            
  • Maturity = 2 → P3 - SIMPLIFY
    • 
            
  • Maturity = 3 → PASS
    • 
            
  • Readiness % = Sum(Weighted) ÷ Sum(Max)
    • 
          

          
Measured cycle days and error rates are shown as measured context only and never enter any score formula.
