One deal, structured several ways at once.

The model behind this tool is the operator’s own working perpetual-deal workbook, rebuilt as a clean web template; the deals loaded in it are fictional samples. The operator brings 15+ years in commercial contracts, deal desk, and revenue operations, working in corporate roles since 2006, and has governed contracts up to $300M and priced 3,000+ SKUs.

      How it works
      
    
      

    
      
      
      
        
      1. Build the options
      
        
      Start with a current deal and a proposed deal—and add more option variants if needed—each built from individual SKU lines compared side by side.
      
      
      
      
      
        
      2. Two kinds of lines
      
        
      SEAT lines price as list price × partner factor × extra factor, with seats able to compound at a yearly growth rate. USAGE/COMMIT lines price a prepaid commit plus overage past the included units, with any unused commitment shown as its own waste line.
      
      
      
      
      
        
      3. The note
      
        
      The approval request is written from the deal as you build it. Any fields you leave blank drop out of the note automatically—copy it and send it.
      
      
      
    
      

    
      
      
      Partner factor — what is left of list after the partner's margin: 0.85 means the partner keeps 15%.
      
      
      Extra factor — a further discount stacked on top, as a multiplier: 0.80 is another 20% off.
      
    
      

    
      The four sample deals are fictional, built to show the shapes this tool handles.
