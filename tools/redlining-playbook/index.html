Redlining Playbook & Clause Fallback Matrix

This page is a working contract redlining playbook: eight commercial clauses, four governed fallback positions each, scored live so a negotiator can evaluate counterparty redlines mid-deal against pre-set boundaries instead of improvising concessions in the room.

Practice Record & Provenance: Built from 15+ years in commercial contracts, deal desk, and revenue operations; contracts up to $300M; 3,000+ SKUs under management. All sample negotiation data on this page is fictional and provided to demonstrate live clause fallback governance.

How the Playbook & Scorer Work

For each of the eight clauses, set the tier the other side is asking for: Tier 0 is the standard position, Tier 1 the Deal Desk fallback, Tier 2 an escalation position, Tier 3 the walk-away boundary. The scorer adds up the risk points — 0, 1, 3, or 10 per clause depending on tier — names the approver each clause now needs, and routes the deal: low-risk redlines fast-track, mid-range redlines stay Deal Desk approvable, and anything touching the walk-away boundary is blocked. Read the playbook matrix alongside to see what each tier gives and gets.
