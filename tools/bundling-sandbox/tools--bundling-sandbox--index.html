This page is a working bundling sandbox: two commercial bundle options built line by line against a buyer's budget ceiling and an internal gross margin floor, each gated live as PASS or FAIL. Compare the raw discounted sales ask against a restructured multi-tier bundle to see how commercial structure — not blind discounting — clears the margin gate inside the buyer's budget.

How It Works

Set the buyer's budget ceiling, your internal margin floor, and the contract term, then build each option line by line: quantity or seats, annual list price, unit cost, and discount. Every line shows its net unit price, 3-year revenue, 3-year cost, and gross margin, and each option is gated live — it passes only if its total revenue fits the buyer's budget and its margin clears your floor. Adjust quantities, list prices, costs, or discounts and watch a failing bundle turn into a passing one without giving margin away blind.

$

Maximum total contract revenue the buyer's procurement ceiling allows.

%

Minimum blended gross margin % required to clear Finance governance.

Yrs

Multiplies recurring license lines; one-time implementation lines never scale with term.

Workbook Modeling Rules

          
Every figure on this page is computed live from the inputs using the workbook's deterministic commercial logic:

          
    
            
  • Net Unit Price = Annual List × (1 − Discount %)
    • 
            
  • Recurring Line: Term Revenue = Qty × Net Price × Term
    • 
            
  • Recurring Line: Term COGS = Qty × Unit COGS × Term
    • 
            
  • One-Time Line: Revenue = Qty × Net Price (no term multiplier)
    • 
            
  • One-Time Line: COGS = Qty × Unit COGS (no term multiplier)
    • 
            
  • Gross Margin % = (Revenue − COGS) ÷ Revenue (0% if Revenue = 0)
    • 
            
  • Gate PASS: Revenue > 0 AND Revenue ≤ Ceiling AND Margin ≥ Floor
    • 
          

          
Implementation lines intentionally carry no term multiplier so multi-year license commitments absorb fixed onboarding cost rather than multiplying it.
