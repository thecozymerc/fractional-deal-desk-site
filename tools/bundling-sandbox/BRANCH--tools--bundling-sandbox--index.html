Commercial Restructuring & Margin Bundling Sandbox.

Enterprise buyers frequently demand aggressive unit discounts on recurring software licenses to hit their internal procurement budget ceiling. When sales teams simply apply a flat discount to meet the buyer's target price, the deal drops below the company's gross margin floor and gets rejected by Finance. This sandbox is the math that answers it: two bundle structures side by side — the raw sales ask and the restructured bundle — every line priced live, and each option gated against the buyer's budget ceiling and the internal margin floor at the same time. All figures, SKUs, and cost baselines in it are sample data.

Directions: Set the buyer's budget ceiling, your internal margin floor, and the contract term, then build each option line by line: quantity or seats, annual list price, unit cost, and discount. Every line shows its net unit price, 3-year revenue, 3-year cost, and gross margin, and each option is gated live — it passes only if its total revenue fits the buyer's budget and its margin clears your floor. Adjust quantities, list prices, costs, or discounts and watch a failing bundle turn into a passing one without giving margin away blind.

Inputs, logic, limits

Inputs: the three deal-level settings (budget ceiling, margin floor %, contract term in years) plus four levers per component line — quantity/seats, annual list $, unit COGS $, and discount %. There is no “service hours” input; implementation is a single priced line. Logic: net unit price is list × (1 − discount); revenue and COGS run over the contract term; gross margin % is (revenue − COGS) ÷ revenue, per line and per option; the gate passes an option only when its total revenue is at or under the ceiling AND its margin is at or over the floor. One deliberate quirk, preserved from the source: each option's implementation line is a one-time item — its revenue and cost carry no term multiplier, unlike the recurring license lines. Limits: sample data only, one sheet in the source.

Story 4 — Restructure the deal, keep the margin.

What this piece shows. A Commercial Restructuring & Margin-Preserving Bundling Sandbox that models side-by-side deal structures. Instead of rejecting the deal or giving away margin, the model restructures the commercial mechanics — adjusting license discount depth, contract term, and professional implementation service bundles — to hit the buyer's target budget while clearing internal gross margin guardrails. The workbook includes an Unstructured Ask (Fails Margin Gate), which shows the raw sales request — heavy flat discount on software licenses — and calculates the exact gross margin shortfall; a Restructured Bundle (Passes Margin Gate), which re-allocates the commercial mix across recurring software tiers, multi-year commitment locks, and fixed-scope implementation services; and a Blended Margin & Buyer Budget Validator: live formulas verifying that (1) the buyer's total spend stays at or below their target price ceiling, and (2) the company's blended gross margin clears the required Finance floor.

The point it proves. It unblocks deadlocked enterprise deals without cutting corners on margin governance, and gives Deal Desk and Sales an objective math sandbox to restructure deals in real time instead of arguing over flat discount exceptions.
