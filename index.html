01 — The Mess I Fix

The deal is not stuck for the reason people say it is.

When a complex deal reaches the finish line and stalls, everyone usually has an explanation. Underneath, the basics are undone.

Paperwork is missing. Information is incomplete. Accounts are unverified. Sometimes the missing piece is the approval itself. Sometimes the check finds the opposite problem: a deal was approved that was not supposed to be.

The runbook exists, but it is too wordy to use in the moment. The process exists, but nobody is following the same version of it. Responsibilities are unclear, so the deal bounces between Deal Desk, Finance, Legal, and Fulfillment until it rots instead of booking.

If nobody holds the line, the deal drops into an endless loop. It gets pushed back, passed around, and slowly dies. That is deal paralysis.

Clean intake is not bureaucracy. It is how the deal gets done right the first time.

That is the mess I fix.

02 — How I Work

The big work is reading the process.

I do not start by blaming the team that touched the deal last. I read the whole process and find where it actually broke.

  1. I ask what we are doing and why. The why drives the action.
  2. I check what the client has now, what the current deal is, whether this is new or a renewal, and whether they are eligible.
  3. I look for the signal: what do the stakeholders actually want?
  4. I check whether the stakeholders did their part. The tell is usually simple stuff: missing docs, missing info.
  5. I find what is blocking the deal. Is something missing, or is a policy in the way?
  6. I run the client’s own checklist in full. If they do not have one, that is the first finding.

If the blocker is policy, the question is practical: can we bend it, or do we need to break it and recreate it in a form that works?

Then I build the fix into the operation — ownership, decision rights, escalation, and consequences — so the same problem does not need a hero the next time.

04 — The Lines I Hold

These rules are stated up front so the wrong clients can screen themselves out before we get on a call.

Clarity over hearsay

Nothing gets approved without clean formulas, contract terms, or policy behind it. If a deal structure cannot be backed by the math and the written record, I will not sign off on it.

Audit-proofing over false speed

Speed that breaks compliance or hides margin loss is not speed. It is a time bomb. Real velocity comes from clean intake and guardrails that work while the deal is moving.

Systems over heroics

Fix the broken process once instead of firefighting it every month. I do not become an administrative concierge for incomplete submissions. I build the calculators, playbooks, and accountability structures so the fix stays fixed.

If you want a yes-man to rubber-stamp bad numbers or hide a discount, I am not your operator. If you want the operation diagnosed honestly and built right the first time, we will work well together.
